# Dicty Community Resource Plugin Terms of Service

These terms apply to use of the Dicty Community Resource ChatGPT plugin and its hosted DCR MCP service.

## Catalog access

The plugin provides read-only access to the Dicty Stock Center catalog for research and education. It can find records and report catalog availability. The Stock Center handles orders, deposits, and record corrections through its own processes.

Catalog entries and availability can change. Check the linked Stock Center record for current information before relying on a result.

## Use of the service

Use the plugin in accordance with applicable law. Do not interfere with the service, attempt to gain unauthorized access, or circumvent its rate limits. Use catalog identifiers and search terms relevant to your lookup. Keep credentials and other confidential information out of lookup requests.

Your use of ChatGPT is also subject to the OpenAI terms applicable to your account.
